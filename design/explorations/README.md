# Design explorations, round 1 and 2

Source of the Design canvas "Tarikh: three design directions". Each `.dc.html` is one artboard.

- `Main`, `ClearDay-*`: direction A, Clear Day (pale, one blue, Geist).
- `NightDesk-*`: direction B, dark with a day-count ruler (Hanken Grotesk, Martian Mono).
- `Seal-*`: direction C, paper and ink with a stamp (Bodoni Moda, Instrument Sans).
- `Flagship`: round 2, "the long table", an interactive date scrubber that recomputes the four clocks. Its script ports the rules in `app/deadlines.py` as corrected by decision 0004 (the complaint month ends on the same calendar date, not the day before).

Sample papers are fictional and live in `samples/`. Nothing here is production code.
