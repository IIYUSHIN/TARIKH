# Tarikh v2: the company, not the feature

Owner feedback on v1 (20 Nov 2026, 19:17): the page was beige throughout, read as a brochure for one feature, and put Azure at the centre. v2 is
built as a real company with several products, a distinct identity and a working multi-screen app.

## Positioning

**Tarikh is the litigation platform for cheque-bounce cases in India.** It reads the papers, counts every statutory deadline,
drafts court-ready documents, tracks every hearing and prices every settlement, for one cheque or ten thousand.

Line: *Tarikh pe tarikh ends here.* Tarikh means "date", the word a court uses when it fixes the next hearing.

Why now (all cited on the site):
- 43,05,932 cheque cases pending, Ministry of Law reply to the Lok Sabha, December 2024.
- 25.9 lakh of them pending more than two years, data tabled July 2025.
- 6,50,283 pending in Delhi district courts alone on 1 September 2025.
- Supreme Court, *Sanjabij Tari v. Kishore S. Borcar* (25 Sep 2025), in force in trial courts from 1 Nov 2025: complaint
  synopsis after the index, the accused's email/phone/WhatsApp on affidavit, online payment by QR/UPI, revised compounding
  costs (0 / 5 / 7.5 / 10 percent by stage), stage-wise pendency dashboards.

## Products

| Product | For | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Tarikh Desk | Advocates, chambers, firms | Matter workspace: read papers, clocks, drafts, hearing diary, client updates |
| Tarikh Recover | NBFCs, banks, distributors, MSMEs | Portfolio of hundreds of bounced cheques: import, stage dashboard, panel advocates, settlements |
| Tarikh Check | Anyone holding a bounced cheque | Free public tool: is my case still in time, what to do this week |
| Tarikh API | Lenders' and collection platforms | The deadline engine and paper reading as an API |

Shared capabilities: Read, Count, Draft, Diary, Settle, Speak, Ask, Vault and audit.

## Identity

- Concept: the Indian tear-off wall calendar. Black weekday numerals, red Sundays, a perforated leaf. Time is the material.
- Colour: black #0B0B0B, white #FFFFFF, warm grey scale, **calendar red #E2321B** as the single signal. Amber only for
  "running short". No beige page backgrounds, no gradients, no purple.
- Type: Schibsted Grotesk (all text, 400 to 900), Anton (calendar numerals only), JetBrains Mono (case data, section numbers),
  Tiro Devanagari Hindi (wordmark तारीख़). All self-hosted.
- Surfaces: alternate black and white bands. Real product UI is the hero image, never illustration.
- Motion: leaf tear (calendar), count-up on cited numbers, settle-in on scroll. All off under reduced motion.

## Honesty rules (unchanged)

No invented customers, logos, testimonials or usage numbers. Market numbers only with a cited public source. Plans are shown
as early-access plans; nothing is on sale. Footer states it is a prototype and not legal advice. Every party in the demo is
fictional. Azure appears where it belongs (Trust, Developers), not as the headline.
