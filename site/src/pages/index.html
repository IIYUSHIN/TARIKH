Ready for the Supreme Court’s new rules.

In Sanjabij Tari v. Kishore S. Borcar the Supreme Court told every trial court how Section 138 cases must now run. Each direction that touches a chambers’ work is built in.

The directionWhat Tarikh does
A synopsis in a set format, right after the index of every complaintDrafts it from the traced facts, in that position.
The accused’s email, phone and messaging details, on affidavit, at filingCollects them at intake and drafts the affidavit.
Summons may be served electronically and by the complainant (dasti)Logs every attempt at service and its result in the diary.
Accused may pay early through a court QR code or UPI linkTracks payment and prepares the compounding application.
Revised compounding costs: 0, 5, 7.5 and 10 percent by stagePrices the settlement for the stage the case is in.
Dashboards of pendency, settlement and adjournments by stageGives lenders and firms the same view of their own portfolio.

Source: Supreme Court of India, Criminal Appeal 1755 of 2010, decided 25 September 2025, 2025 INSC 1158.
