The problem, without an adjective

Miss one and the complaint can be lost.

A Section 138 case is four periods that start one after another. Each begins on a date that is printed on one paper and not on another. Counting them by hand, from three scans, is where files go wrong.

Where AI is used, and where it is not

A model reads. A rule counts. A person decides.

Used for

  • Reading the papersCheque, memo and notice become fields with a place on the page.
  • Hearing the clientA spoken account in English, Hindi or Punjabi becomes text.
  • Hiding personal detailsNames, numbers and addresses are masked before any model sees the account.
  • Drafting from factsA short chronology, using only facts that trace to a paper.
  • Answering about one fileAn assistant that can only read that matter’s facts and its dates.

Never used for

  • Counting datesA rule engine with written tests does it, the same way every time.
  • Deciding what the law isThe desk states its rules and its doubts. The advocate decides.
  • Filing anythingThere is no connection to any court or e-filing system.
  • Sending anythingNo notice, email or message leaves the desk.
  • Predicting an outcomeIt does not say who will win, and it will not be taught to.
