Questions people ask first.

Why per matter and not per seat?

A chambers with two advocates may run two hundred cheque matters. Charging per lawyer would make the busiest, smallest offices pay the least for the most work, and lenders the most for the least.

Is Tarikh a law firm?

No. Tarikh is software for advocates and for the people who instruct them. It does not appear in court, give legal advice or decide anything about a case.

Where does our data live?

On Microsoft Azure, in the region the account is set to. Personal details in a client’s account are masked before any model drafts from it. See Trust for the full picture.

Can we try it with our own matters?

Yes, on your own copy of Tarikh. Create an account and add matters, or load the sample matters first. Until Tarikh has had an independent security review, keep real client papers on a copy you run yourself.
