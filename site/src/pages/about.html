About

A date is a small thing to lose a case over.

Section 138 cases turn on arithmetic. A cheque is returned, and a chain of periods starts: three months, thirty days, fifteen days, one month. Each one begins on a date written on a different piece of paper, and miscounting any of them can cost the complaint.

Tarikh is a case desk built around that fact. It reads the papers, shows where each date came from, counts the clocks by rule and drafts a brief for an advocate to check. The AI reads and drafts. It does not count, decide or file.

The name is the Hindi and Urdu word for a date, and the word a court gives when it fixes the next hearing.

What it is today

A working prototype and a design for a service. It has no customers, and this site shows no figures, quotes or logos because there are none to show. The code, the decisions behind it and the open questions are all in the public repository.
