    What happens in a cheque case

    Four deadlines, one after another.

    3months

    to deposit the cheque, from the date written on it.

    30days

    to send a written demand, from the day the memo reached you.

    15days

    for the drawer to pay, from the day after they receive it.

    1month

    to file the complaint, from the day after those 15 days end.

    Do I need an advocate to send the notice?

    The law does not say a notice must come from an advocate, but most are sent through one, and a badly drafted notice can weaken the case. Tarikh Check tells you the deadline; an advocate can make sure the notice itself holds up.

    What if the drawer pays part of the amount?

    Part payment can change what you can claim. Keep a record of every payment, and tell your advocate before the notice goes out.

    The deadline has passed. Is it over?

    Not always. A court can accept a late complaint if there was sufficient cause for the delay. A cheque that is still within its three months may also be deposited again. Both need an advocate’s judgment.
