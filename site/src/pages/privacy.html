Privacy

Short, because there is little to say.

Last updated 20 November 2026.

This website

It sets no cookies. It loads no analytics, advertising or third-party scripts and no third-party fonts: every font is served from the site itself. It does not know who you are.

Like any web host, the server that delivers the pages may keep ordinary access logs, such as the time and address of a request. Tarikh does not read them to identify anyone.

The sample desk

The sample matters are fictional. The demo accepts no uploads and stores nothing. The dates you move on the table exist only in your browser until you close the page.

The contact form

It sends nothing. It prepares a GitHub issue draft that you submit yourself, and public issues are visible to everyone. Do not put a real client’s details in it.

The product

How the desk treats real papers, the client’s account and Azure services is on the security page. Because this is a prototype, please do not use it with real client material.
