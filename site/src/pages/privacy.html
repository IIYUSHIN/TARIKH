Privacy

Short, because there is little to say.

Last updated 20 November 2026.

This website

It sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising or third-party scripts or fonts. Every font is served from the site itself.

Tarikh Check

The dates you enter are counted in your browser. They are not sent or stored anywhere.

Your account

Your name, email and a scrypt hash of your password are stored on the server that runs Tarikh, with the matters you create. Your password itself is never stored. Sample matters are fictional.

Early access

The form sends nothing. It prepares a public GitHub issue that you submit yourself. Do not include real client details.

The product

How Tarikh handles papers, recordings and personal details is described on the Trust page. Please do not use the prototype with real client material.
