Company

A date is a small thing to lose a case over.

Tarikh means “date”. It is the word a court gives when it fixes the next hearing, and the word litigants say with a sigh.

Cheque-bounce cases are the largest single block of pending criminal cases in India’s magistrate courts. Most are not legally hard. They are hard to run: four deadlines in a row, each counted from a different paper, followed by years of hearings, adjournments and settlement talks.

Tarikh is built on one idea. The parts that are arithmetic should be exact and explained. The parts that are reading and drafting can be helped by AI, provided every fact keeps its source. The parts that are judgment stay with an advocate.

We start with Section 138 because it is narrow, high-volume and unforgiving of mistakes. The same engine can later carry other time-bound proceedings.
