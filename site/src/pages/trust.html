AI is used for

  • Reading papersCheques, memos and notices become fields with a place on the page.
  • Hearing the clientSpeech in English, Hindi or Punjabi becomes text.
  • Masking personal detailsBefore any drafting model sees a client’s account.
  • Drafting from traced factsEvery fact in a draft links to its source, or it is removed.
  • Answering about one fileThrough two read-only tools, and nothing else.

AI is never used for

  • Counting a dateA tested rule engine does it, the same way every time.
  • Deciding the lawTarikh states its rules and its doubts. The advocate decides.
  • Filing or sendingTarikh has no connection to any court and sends nothing on its own.
  • Predicting an outcomeNo “chance of winning”, and none will be added.
  • Scoring a personParties appear as the papers name them. Nobody is rated.

Where data goes

Five steps, and what crosses each one.

  1. 1

    Upload

    Papers and recordings are stored with the matter, in the account’s own storage.

  2. 2

    Read

    Page images go to Azure Content Understanding and come back as fields.

  3. 3

    Hear

    Audio goes to Azure Speech for text. The recording stays with the chambers.

  4. 4

    Mask

    Azure AI Language finds names, numbers and addresses and masks them.

  5. 5

    Draft

    Only masked text and traced facts reach the Azure AI Foundry model.

Identity

Tarikh signs in to Azure with Microsoft Entra ID through the standard credential chain. There is no API key in the code, the repository or a settings file.

Least access for the assistant

The question-answering agent runs on Azure AI Foundry Agent Service with two read-only tools scoped to one matter. It has no tool to write, send, browse or open another matter.

Approval and audit

A draft leaves draft only when a named advocate approves it. The approval records who, when and which version of the facts. The log is append-only.

This website

No cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party scripts or fonts. Tarikh Check runs in your browser and sends nothing.

Responsible AI, in practice

  • Fairness: no scoring or ranking of people.
  • Reliability: dates from tested rules, not models.
  • Privacy: masking before drafting.
  • Inclusiveness: three spoken languages; every control works by keyboard; no meaning by colour alone.
  • Transparency: every fact shows its source; every rule shows its standing.
  • Accountability: a named person approves; the log keeps it.

What we will not claim

Tarikh is a prototype. It has not had an independent security review, has no customers yet, and should not be used with real client papers. When that changes, this page will say so.
